FICTIONAL SAMPLE - Request for Quotation: Document Intelligence Services

C.1 Background
The agency receives about 40,000 grant reports per year and wants to speed up their review.

C.2 Technical Requirements
C.2.1 The contractor shall provide a document classification service that sorts incoming grant reports into at least 12 report types.
C.2.2 The solution must extract key fields, including award number, reporting period and total expenditures, with documented accuracy of at least 95 percent.
C.2.3 The contractor shall host the solution in a FedRAMP Moderate authorized cloud environment.
C.2.4 All data must be encrypted at rest and in transit using FIPS 140-validated cryptography.
C.2.5 The system shall provide a human review queue so that staff can correct low-confidence extractions before they are saved.

C.3 Responsible AI
C.3.1 The contractor shall deliver model documentation describing training data, evaluation results and known limitations.
C.3.2 The contractor must test extraction accuracy separately for scanned and born-digital documents and report any performance gaps.
C.3.3 The contractor shall not use agency data to train models offered to other customers.

C.4 Management
C.4.1 The contractor will provide monthly status reports, including accuracy metrics and open issues.
C.4.2 Key personnel shall include a project manager and a machine learning lead with at least five years of experience.
C.4.3 The contractor shall provide a transition plan that allows the agency to export all data and configurations at contract end.
