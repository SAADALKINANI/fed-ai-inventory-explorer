Our DocSort platform classifies incoming grant reports into 15 configurable report types using a fine-tuned transformer model. In a pilot with a similar agency it reached 97 percent classification accuracy.

Field extraction covers the award number, reporting period, total expenditures and 20 other fields. Our documented extraction accuracy on government forms is 96 percent, measured on a held-out test set of 5,000 documents.

The platform runs in our FedRAMP Moderate authorized cloud environment. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3, using FIPS 140-validated cryptographic modules.

Low-confidence extractions are routed to a human review queue where agency staff can correct values before they are saved to the system of record.

We will deliver a model card describing training data sources, evaluation results, and known limitations. We test accuracy separately for scanned documents and born-digital documents and will report any performance gap between them in each monthly report.

Agency data is used only for this agency's models and is never used to train models offered to other customers.

Monthly status reports will include accuracy metrics, volumes processed and open issues. Our key personnel are a certified project manager and a machine learning lead with eight years of experience.

At contract end we provide a transition plan and full export of all data, model configurations and review history in open formats.
